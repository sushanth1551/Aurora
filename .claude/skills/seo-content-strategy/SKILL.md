# seo-content-strategy

**Description:** Turn research evidence into a measurable content plan.

## Purpose
Develop a strategy connecting audience needs, evidence, keywords, and editorial
goals without guaranteeing search outcomes.

## When to use it
Use after research and keyword evidence is available or when producing a
clearly labeled strategy hypothesis.

## Required inputs
- Business goals, audience, market, and locale.
- Research findings and their sources.
- Keyword clusters or an explicit note that keyword evidence is missing.

## Execution workflow
1. Confirm goal, audience, and constraints.
2. Map evidence and intent to content opportunities.
3. Define topic clusters, formats, and proposed internal relationships.
4. Prioritize by rationale, effort, and evidence strength.
5. Define measurable outcomes and review assumptions with the user.

## Expected outputs
Prioritized themes, audience needs, keyword mapping, proposed formats, KPIs,
and evidence gaps.

## Error handling
Escalate conflicting goals or insufficient evidence; label assumptions rather
than presenting them as established facts.

## Quality requirements
Recommend useful, original content for people first; disclose uncertainty; do
not imply guaranteed rankings or traffic.
