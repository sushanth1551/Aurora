# fact-verification

**Description:** Check factual claims against attributable evidence.

## Purpose
Assess whether claims are supported, contradicted, ambiguous, or unverified.

## When to use it
Use before research summaries, reports, or drafted content present factual
claims as reliable.

## Required inputs
- Claims or content to review.
- Candidate sources and provenance.
- Relevant date, locale, and standard of evidence.

## Execution workflow
1. Extract atomic, checkable claims.
2. Locate direct evidence and record source dates and context.
3. Compare source quality, independence, and agreement.
4. Classify each claim and explain the evidence.
5. Recommend revision or further research for unresolved claims.

## Expected outputs
Claim-by-claim status, source references, rationale, confidence, and suggested
corrections or follow-up.

## Error handling
If evidence cannot be accessed or is stale, label the claim unverified. Do not
convert absence of evidence into proof of falsity.

## Quality requirements
Preserve uncertainty and context, avoid fabricated citations, and distinguish
primary sources from secondary reporting.
