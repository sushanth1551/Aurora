# seo-quality-audit

**Description:** Review content against explicit SEO and editorial criteria.

## Purpose
Identify actionable content and technical-on-page issues while separating
observable checks from subjective recommendations.

## When to use it
Use on a supplied page, draft, or content brief when audit scope is clear.

## Required inputs
- Content or page URL/text.
- Target audience, locale, and primary topic if available.
- Audit criteria or permission to apply a stated baseline rubric.

## Execution workflow
1. Record scope and input limitations.
2. Check clarity, structure, intent fit, accessibility, and on-page signals.
3. Check factual claims against supplied evidence when available.
4. Rank findings by impact and confidence and propose actionable edits.
5. Distinguish missing data from a detected defect.

## Expected outputs
Prioritized findings, evidence or examples, severity/confidence, and practical
recommendations.

## Error handling
Report inaccessible pages or absent metadata; do not infer technical findings
from page copy alone.

## Quality requirements
Avoid keyword-density prescriptions and ranking guarantees. Keep findings
specific, reproducible, and proportionate to evidence.
